# Clean: style rules

Approved by Varun on 2026-10-31. Light and minimal; colour only where it means something.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Off-white page with a faint dot grid | `#F6F5F1` |
| Text | Ink black / muted grey | `#16171B` / `#6B6F7B` |
| Colour 1 | Cobalt blue | `#2F54EB` |
| Colour 2 | Orange | `#F76707` |
| Accent | Green (highlighted words) | `#0CA678` |

- **Type:** Inter. Headlines 800 weight with tight letter-spacing; subtitles 400 in grey; kickers small caps-spaced in colour.
- **Surfaces:** white cards, soft two-layer shadow, rounded corners, a coloured rule on top for compare cards. No glow, no bloom.
- **Motion:** quiet: words rise in one by one, rows slide in, numbers count up with a small pulse, underlines draw
  themselves under headlines; section breaks use a colour-1 cover wipe; light confetti only on the end card.
- **Particles** are paint, not light (`particleBlend: "normal"`): keep them few and small.
- Light grain (multiply, ~2.5%) so flat areas don't band after YouTube's re-encode.
