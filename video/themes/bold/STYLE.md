# Bold: style rules

Approved by Varun on 2026-10-31. Flat and loud: stickers, capitals and bounce.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | Electric indigo with big flat shapes drifting behind (15% opacity) | `#3B2BFF` |
| Text | White / lavender | `#FFFFFF` / `#CFC9FF` |
| Ink (outlines, shadows, text on cards) | Near-black | `#0D0B24` |
| Colour 1 | Yellow | `#FFD60A` |
| Colour 2 | Pink | `#FF5FA2` |
| Accent | Mint | `#00E5A0` |

- **Type:** Archivo Black in capitals for headlines, titles and numbers (with a hard ink offset shadow); Space Grotesk for body text.
- **Surfaces:** sticker cards (white or slot colour) with thick ink outlines and hard offset shadows; no gradients, no glow.
- **Motion:** bouncy (`back.out`, elastic bars, `jelly` squash and stretch); list items drop in with physics and settle
  in place; numbers drop from above; band wipes for section breaks; confetti on big numbers and the end card (keep it
  small enough that the number stays readable).
- **Particles** are paint (`particleBlend: "normal"`).
